# Art credits

All 3D models in `Assets/Art` are by **Kenney** (https://www.kenney.nl) and released under
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**: http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
They are free for personal, educational and commercial use; attribution is appreciated but not required.
Each pack's original `License.txt` is kept in its folder.

| Folder | Pack | Source | License | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kenney_CarKit | Car Kit 3.1 | https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit | CC0 | Player car, parked/obstacle cars, cones, wheels |
| Kenney_CityKitRoads | City Kit (Roads) | https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads | CC0 | Street lights, stop signs, barriers, traffic light |
| Kenney_CityKitSuburban | City Kit (Suburban) | https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban | CC0 | Houses around the lot, fences, planters, trees |
| Kenney_NatureKit | Nature Kit | https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit | CC0 | Trees, bushes, rocks, grass |

Only a curated subset of each pack is included to keep the repository small (~2 MB).
Procedural textures (asphalt, painted lines, grass) and the cockpit dashboard mesh are generated in code by this project.
